Effects Lab loop library

Every loop shipped with Effects Lab is synthesized by src/loops.js when the page
runs. There are no sampled recordings and no third-party audio files.

That follows the same idea as the licenses/ folder in the sibling project
https://github.com/hckshr/session-drummer : anything that can be heard is named,
and its license is written down. These loops are original to this repository.

  Clean chords   plucked chord bed, two bars at 96 BPM
  Guitar riff    single-note Karplus-Strong line
  Arpeggio       picked single notes
  Bass line      simple oscillator bass
  Drum beat      synthesized kick, snare, and hats
  Soft keys      detuned sine pad
  Synth lead     band-limited melody

You can copy, modify, and redistribute them with the app. No attribution to
an outside sample library is required, because none was used.

A guitar take the user records is their own performance. It stays in
IndexedDB on their device under the database name "effects-lab-takes" and
is not uploaded.
